No account, no server

Your habits live on your device — and, if your iPhone is signed into iCloud, in your own private iCloud as well. Nothing to set up, no sign-in inside the app, no server of ours anywhere in the path.

Calendar, widgets, Siri

Reminders are scheduled by the device itself — local, no push server.
